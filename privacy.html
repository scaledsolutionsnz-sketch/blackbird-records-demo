What we collect, why we collect it, and what we do not do with it. Short version: we are a record shop, not a data business.

1. Who we are

Blackbird Records is a record store and cafe at 132 Jellicoe Street, Te Puke 3119, Bay of Plenty, New Zealand. This policy explains how we handle personal information, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

  • When you contact us. Your name, phone number or email address, and whatever you tell us, so we can answer you or hold something aside.
  • When you buy something. Payment is handled by our card terminal provider. We do not see or store your full card details.
  • When you visit this site. Standard technical information such as browser type and pages viewed, collected by our hosting provider to keep the site running.

This website has no contact form and no account sign up. If you have not phoned, emailed or spoken to us, we do not hold anything about you beyond that basic technical information.

3. Why we hold it

To reply to enquiries, to keep track of records people have asked us to look out for, to process a sale or a special order, and to meet our record keeping obligations. Nothing else.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not add you to a mailing list because you rang the shop. We do not build a profile of you from your purchases.

5. Third party services

A few parts of this site rely on services run by other companies, and those companies have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps for the embedded map showing where the shop is.
  • Google Fonts for the typefaces used on this site.
  • Pexels for photography served on this site.
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok if you follow the links to our social profiles.
  • Our website host for serving these pages.

6. Cookies

We do not set our own tracking or advertising cookies. Embedded third party content, such as the Google map, may set its own cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though the map may then stop working.

7. Keeping it safe

We keep contact details only as long as we need them and take reasonable steps to protect them. Enquiry emails sit in our normal business email account, which is password protected.

8. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Ring or email and we will sort it out, usually on the spot.

9. Children

This site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. To ask anything about it, call the shop on 021 101 0598 or send us an email.
