What is in the shop

Records, discs, and something out of the coffee machine

Blackbird is two things in one room. A record store with new and second-hand stock, and a cafe run by someone who has done it before in this town.

01

New vinyl

New releases and reissues, sealed and priced as they land. If it is out and you want it, ask us and we will chase it up.

02

Second-hand bins

Every second-hand record is cleaned, graded and priced by hand before it hits the bins. Restocked constantly, so the dig is never the same twice.

03

CDs

The format that never actually went away. Racks of second-hand discs alongside new titles, including a lot of albums that were never pressed on vinyl.

04

The coffee side

Espresso, and something out of the cabinet to go with it. Angela ran Marigold Cafe here in Te Puke before Blackbird, so the coffee is not an afterthought.

05

Listening room

Headphone stations in the lounge and a separate listening room out the back. Sit down with a record first, then decide whether it comes home with you.

06

New Zealand & independent

Real shelf space for local and independent artists, from Dunedin pressings to Bay of Plenty bands dropping off their own CDs. Ask and we will point them out.

Since 2024

Our story

A family shop on Jellicoe Street

Blackbird Records opened in April 2024, run by Justin and Angela Bruning with their kids Eliza and Murphy in and out of the place. Justin and Angela had already spent years feeding this town at Marigold Cafe, so when the chance came to build something around records, the coffee machine came with them.

The result is a shop you can spend an hour in. New pressings up front, second-hand bins that took months of cleaning and pricing before they opened, CDs along the wall, cassettes, retro music posters and artwork, and a few gifty bits and plants scattered through. Locals treat it as a stop, travellers heading through to the Mount or Rotorua treat it as a find.

You don't know what you are looking for until you see it. Justin Bruning, Te Puke Times
